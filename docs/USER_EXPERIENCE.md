# GospelLift UX and Brain Activator Guide

Use plain, friendly language. Assume the artist may be new to digital promotion. Explain unfamiliar terms with examples; allow blank answers and “Not sure yet.” Suggestions are editable ideas, never claims about the artist. Never invent a testimony, award, collaboration, church affiliation, location or audience statistic.

## Artist journey
1. Read the offer and limitations.
2. Use Brain Activator topics: artist name, song title, genre, goal and song story.
3. Fill only known details; use prompts to think through missing answers.
4. Generate a draft bio/caption and review every sentence for truth.
5. Copy or download the brief.
6. In the future secure product, register, accept privacy/rights terms, upload privately, track status and approve assets before publication.

## Prompt examples
- Name: What name would you like listeners to remember? Is it easy to say and search?
- Title: Check the CD cover or ask the songwriter/producer.
- Story: What inspired the song? What do you hope listeners feel or remember?
- Goal: Reach listeners, prepare for release, promote a video, find event opportunities, or build your brand?
- Genre: Choose the closest fit. “Gospel” or “Not sure yet” is okay.
