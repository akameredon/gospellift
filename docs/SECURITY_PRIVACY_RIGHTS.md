# GospelLift Security, Privacy and Rights Baseline

## Before accepting real uploads
- Configure Supabase project, email auth, private audio bucket and RLS/storage policies.
- Test two independent artist accounts plus an anonymous browser.
- Verify artists can access only their own files and permitted fields; admin access must be server-enforced.
- Keep service-role secrets out of browser code and repository.
- Define retention, backup/restore, deletion and incident response.
- Replace draft privacy/terms with accurate operator contact details and reviewed terms.

## Music and artwork rights
Artist confirms they own or are authorized to submit the recording, composition, lyrics, artwork and photos. Store consent and statement version. Submission does not grant ownership to GospelLift. Obtain explicit approval for every asset/channel before publication. Pause disputed content and provide a takedown contact/process.

## AI and third parties
Do not submit masters to external AI systems or use content for model training without separate informed consent, clear provider/purpose/retention disclosures and cost disclosure where relevant.

## Ethical promotion
No bots, fake engagement, spam, impersonation, deceptive claims or guaranteed results. Record real metrics with source and date range. Third-party campaign expenses require prior approval.

## Current site limitation
The static preview does not upload audio or transmit form answers. Privacy and terms pages are drafts, not a certification of legal compliance.
