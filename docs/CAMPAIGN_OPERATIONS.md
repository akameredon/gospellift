# GospelLift 14-Day Starter Campaign SOP

## Proposed package
One artist, one lead song, 14 days. Service fee: ₦25,000 for the agreed scope. Third-party and out-of-pocket expenses are separate and must be disclosed and approved before spending.

## Intake checklist
- Artist/stage name and contact route
- Song title, genre, language, release status/date
- Digital master or physical CD digitization plan
- Artist bio, background, intended message and target audience
- Existing links and artist-owned social accounts
- Cover art/photo and permissions
- Rights declaration for recording, composition, lyrics, artwork and photos
- Exact deliverables, schedule and approved expense budget

## Timeline
- Days 1–2: intake and rights check.
- Days 3–4: human listening review and positioning.
- Days 5–7: prepare agreed artwork, short clip and caption drafts.
- Days 8–12: artist-approved, respectful organic sharing/outreach on available channels.
- Days 13–14: report work completed and source-backed results.

Separate outputs from outcomes. Never invent metrics or promise virality. If the operator has no established audience, disclose that. For CD digitization, use a computer optical drive or external USB CD/DVD drive; rip to MP3 for sharing or WAV for uncompressed preservation, verify playback, and keep the original CD.
