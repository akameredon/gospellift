Rights

Artists must own or have permission to submit recordings, compositions, lyrics, artwork, photos and other materials. This static preview does not upload selected audio.

Submitting a song does not automatically authorize public release. Artists should approve every promotional asset and publication channel before use.

The proposed initial package service fee is ₦25,000 for the agreed deliverables. Paid advertising, influencers, radio, filming, travel, printing, distribution and other third-party expenses are separate and require prior approval.

No guaranteed results

GospelLift does not guarantee streams, views, followers, fame, playlist placement, radio play, sales or revenue.

Respectful promotion

No fake engagement, bots, spam, impersonation or deceptive claims. Promotion must follow platform rules.
