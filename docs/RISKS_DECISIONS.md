# GospelLift Risks, Assumptions and Decisions

## Decisions
- Working name: GospelLift; check brand/domain availability before major promotion.
- Brain Activator is a guided thinking aid; suggestions are clearly labeled and editable.
- Current public preview is static and browser-only, not a secure submission backend.
- Private master audio is the default for future uploads.
- Human approval is required before publication.
- No guaranteed views, streams, fame, playlist placement or revenue.
- ₦25,000 is the proposed service fee; third-party costs are separate and pre-approved.

## Risks and mitigations
- Unauthorized music: rights declaration and takedown process.
- Privacy leakage: private storage, least privilege, RLS and cross-account tests.
- Misleading AI: explain limitations and use human review.
- Unapproved expenses: written quote and prior approval.
- No established audience: be transparent and use channels actually available.
- Scope creep: prioritize secure submission before automation.
- Incorrect generated bio: label as draft and never fabricate facts.
- Free-tier limits: verify provider limits and monitor usage.
